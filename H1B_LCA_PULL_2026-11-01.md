## What each filing contains

- `WAGE_RATE_OF_PAY_FROM` is the legally binding minimum the employer will pay that worker.
  `WAGE_RATE_OF_PAY_TO` is an optional ceiling, often very wide (Anthropic files many at $187,574 to $500,000 or $850,000).
- `PW_WAGE_LEVEL` I to IV is the government's seniority bucket for the prevailing wage. This is the
  level axis skillmarketcap does not have today.
- Base salary only. No equity, no bonus.

## Compared with the site's posted-band medians

Posted-band medians at ship time (2026-08-22): Backend systems $363K, TypeScript/JavaScript $324K,
Python $315K, AI/ML $313K. H-1B software engineering at the same companies: floor $188K, midpoint $267K.
The posted midpoints run roughly $50K to $100K above the H-1B midpoints. Not a like-for-like
comparison (different role mix, different time window, filed ranges are also ranges), so treat it as a
direction, not a number to publish.
